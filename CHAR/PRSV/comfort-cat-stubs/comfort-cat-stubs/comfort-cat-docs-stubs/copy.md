# Comfort Cat Copy Guidelines

## Voice & Tone
- **Calm and confident** - Like a cat who knows exactly what they're doing
- **Minimalist** - Every word serves a purpose
- **Helpful** - Guide users without overwhelming them
- **Playful but professional** - A gentle purr in the technical world

## Writing Style
- **Short sentences** - Easy to scan and understand
- **Active voice** - "The system monitors" not "Monitoring is performed"
- **Present tense** - "The pipeline is running" not "The pipeline was running"
- **Concrete language** - "200ms batches" not "fast processing"

## Key Phrases
- **"Sleep easy"** - Reassurance that the system is working
- **"We've got the signal"** - Confidence in monitoring capabilities
- **"Cat Nap Control Room"** - The main brand name
- **"Gentle monitoring"** - Describing the system's approach

## Call-to-Action Guidelines
- **Primary CTAs**: "Start Monitoring", "View Dashboard", "Check Status"
- **Secondary CTAs**: "Learn More", "View Details", "Configure"
- **Destructive CTAs**: "Stop Pipeline", "Reset Configuration"
- **Use action verbs** - Start, View, Check, Configure, Monitor

## Error Messages
- **Be specific** - "SigNoz is unreachable" not "Connection failed"
- **Offer solutions** - "Check if SigNoz is running on port 8080"
- **Stay calm** - "Let's check the connection" not "ERROR: CRITICAL FAILURE"
- **Use the cat metaphor** - "The cat is taking a break" for maintenance

## Status Messages
- **Success**: "All systems purring", "Pipeline healthy", "Monitoring active"
- **Warning**: "Gentle alert", "Something to watch", "Minor hiccup"
- **Error**: "Needs attention", "Connection lost", "Service down"
- **Info**: "Just so you know", "FYI", "Status update"

## Technical Writing
- **Explain the why** - Not just what, but why it matters
- **Use analogies** - "Like a cat's whiskers sensing the environment"
- **Break down complexity** - Step-by-step explanations
- **Provide context** - How this fits into the bigger picture

## Accessibility
- **Clear headings** - Descriptive and hierarchical
- **Alt text** - Describe images and charts meaningfully
- **Link text** - Descriptive, not just "click here"
- **Form labels** - Clear and helpful
