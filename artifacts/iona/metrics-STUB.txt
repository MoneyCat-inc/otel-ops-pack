BossCat SLO metric expectations (edit in scripts/bosscat-alerts-slo-burnrate.ps1):
  • Request counter: http_server_request_duration_seconds_count
  • Latency buckets: http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket
  • Service selector: service="frontend" (or set -ServiceSelector)
If these metrics are not present, either align names to your app or emit matching series via your app/exporter.
