# Progress Dashboard User Guide

## Overview

The Progress Dashboard is your personal voice practice analytics center. It provides insights into your vocal development over time, helping you track improvements in pitch accuracy, expressiveness, resonance balance, and vocal safety.

## Key Features

### 📊 **Trend Visualization**
- **Daily, Weekly, Monthly Views**: See your progress across different time scales
- **Trend Sparklines**: Mini-charts showing your improvement trajectory
- **Delta Indicators**: See how your current performance compares to previous periods

### 🎯 **Core Metrics**

#### **Pitch Accuracy**
- **What it measures**: Percentage of time spent in your target pitch range
- **What higher means**: You're hitting your target pitch more consistently
- **Trend interpretation**: ↗️ Improving means you're getting more accurate over time

#### **Expressiveness**
- **What it measures**: Variety and natural expression in your pitch delivery
- **What higher means**: More dynamic and engaging vocal delivery
- **Trend interpretation**: ↗️ Improving means you're developing more vocal variety

#### **Resonance Balance**
- **What it measures**: Which part of your vocal tract you're using most
- **Options**: Front (bright), Central (balanced), Back (warm)
- **Trend interpretation**: Shows your resonance preferences and development

#### **Safety Timeline**
- **What it measures**: Vocal strain events and safety patterns
- **Color coding**: 
  - 🟢 Green: Low risk (minimal strain)
  - 🟡 Yellow: Moderate risk (some strain)
  - 🔴 Red: High risk (frequent strain)
- **Trend interpretation**: ↘️ Improving means you're practicing more safely

### 🎛️ **Controls & Filters**

#### **Time Period Selection**
- **Last 7 days**: Recent progress snapshot
- **Last 14 days**: Short-term trends
- **Last 30 days**: Monthly overview (default)
- **All time**: Complete practice history

#### **Metric Toggles**
- **Show/Hide Metrics**: Focus on the metrics that matter most to you
- **Customizable View**: Create your personalized dashboard

## Understanding Your Data

### **Privacy & Local Storage**
- **100% Local**: All data stays on your device
- **No Uploads**: Your practice data never leaves your computer
- **Secure**: No cloud storage or external servers involved

### **Data Sources**
- **Practice Sessions**: Data from your voice practice drills
- **Real-time Analysis**: Metrics calculated during practice
- **Session Summaries**: Aggregated data from each practice session

### **Schema Versioning**
- **Automatic Updates**: Data format evolves with app improvements
- **Backward Compatibility**: Old data remains accessible
- **Graceful Migration**: Seamless transitions between versions

## Interpreting Trends

### **Improving Trends (↗️)**
- **Pitch Accuracy**: You're hitting your target range more often
- **Expressiveness**: Your voice is becoming more dynamic and engaging
- **Safety**: You're practicing with less vocal strain

### **Stable Trends (→)**
- **Consistent Performance**: You're maintaining your current level
- **Plateau Period**: Normal part of skill development
- **Consider**: Trying new exercises or techniques

### **Declining Trends (↘️)**
- **Temporary Dip**: Normal variation in practice
- **Fatigue**: May indicate over-practice or strain
- **Consider**: Taking a break or adjusting your practice routine

## Best Practices

### **Regular Check-ins**
- **Weekly Reviews**: Check your progress every week
- **Monthly Deep Dives**: Analyze longer-term trends monthly
- **Goal Setting**: Use trends to set realistic improvement targets

### **Data Interpretation**
- **Look for Patterns**: Identify what practice methods work best
- **Safety First**: Monitor strain events to prevent vocal injury
- **Celebrate Progress**: Acknowledge improvements, no matter how small

### **Troubleshooting**
- **No Data**: Start practicing to generate progress data
- **Missing Metrics**: Some metrics require specific practice types
- **Unexpected Trends**: Consider external factors (health, stress, etc.)

## Troubleshooting

### **Common Issues**

#### **"No Progress Data Yet"**
- **Cause**: No practice sessions recorded
- **Solution**: Complete a practice session to generate data

#### **"Unable to Load Progress"**
- **Cause**: Data loading error
- **Solution**: Click "Try Again" or refresh the page

#### **Missing Metrics**
- **Cause**: Insufficient data for calculation
- **Solution**: Practice more to generate sufficient data points

#### **Unexpected Values**
- **Cause**: Data corruption or calculation error
- **Solution**: Clear browser data and restart practice

### **Data Recovery**
- **Export Data**: Use the data export feature to backup your progress
- **Clear Data**: Use the delete feature to start fresh if needed
- **Restore Data**: Import previously exported data if available
