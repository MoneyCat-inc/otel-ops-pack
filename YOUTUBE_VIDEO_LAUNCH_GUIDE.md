## 🎵 **Audio Guidelines**

### **Voiceover**
- **Tone**: Professional, warm, accessible
- **Pace**: Clear and measured (not rushed)
- **Accent**: Neutral, inclusive
- **Volume**: Consistent, well-balanced

### **Music (Optional)**
- **Style**: Uplifting, professional, subtle
- **Volume**: Low background level
- **Duration**: Match video length
- **License**: Royalty-free or original

### **Accessibility**
- **Closed captions**: Enable auto-generated + manual review
- **Audio description**: Consider for visual elements
- **Transcript**: Provide in description
- **Sign language**: Consider for future versions

## 📱 **Technical Specifications**

### **Video Format**
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Codec**: H.264 or H.265
- **Bitrate**: 8-12 Mbps for 1080p

### **Audio Format**
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit or 24-bit
- **Channels**: Stereo
- **Codec**: AAC or MP3
- **Bitrate**: 128-320 kbps

### **File Size**
- **Target**: Under 500MB for 60-second video
- **Compression**: Use YouTube's recommended settings
- **Upload**: Allow time for processing
